# Mechanical

Status: placeholder.

This folder is for enclosure, mounting, sealing, connector placement, and vibration notes.

No enclosure, mount, or connector family is final yet.

Design goals:

- Survive motorcycle vibration and heat.
- Keep HV clearance and creepage visible and inspectable.
- Separate low-voltage controller access from HV power-board hazards.
- Support small single-cylinder motorcycle installations without becoming brand-specific.
